Here is to your next chapter, your current one, and every version of you still to come. Happy birthday, Plk.

The Apology Letter
Rachit's formal public statement, in print.
By Rachit (reluctantly)  |  28 May 2026
Official Public Apology

Sources close to the matter have confirmed that on the morning of Plk's birthday — a day that should be circled in every calendar, tattooed on every arm, and celebrated with a national holiday — Rachit did not send a wish. Not a text. Not a "hbd". Nothing. Radio silence.

Eyewitness reports indicate that Plk had to remind Rachit himself. That's right — the birthday person sent the reminder. The investigation is ongoing. Legal counsel has been retained.

Dear Plk,
This is my official apology in print: you deserved the first wish, the loudest wish, and the most carefully remembered wish. I missed the timing, and that was completely on me.

You should never have had to remind me on your own birthday. I am sorry for making a day that should have felt effortless feel even slightly less special. You matter to me, and I should have shown that better.

So here it is, properly and publicly: happy birthday, Plk. I hope this year is kinder to you, softer with you, and full of the kind of happiness you never have to chase. Thank you for being you.

In the interim, this newspaper has placed Rachit on a formal watchlist. All future birthdays of Plk have been pre-scheduled in his calendar with reminders starting 30 days in advance.

"I forgot. I know. I'm sorry. Please forgive me. Also, happy birthday, Plk."

— Rachit, writing this with genuine remorse

In a brief statement released to this newspaper, Rachit said: "In my defence... actually, I have no defence. Plk is Plk. She deserved a wish the moment she woke up. I failed. This whole newspaper is my apology. Please be kind."

The Birthday Times editorial board has reviewed his statement and finds it partially acceptable. Verdict: still on probation.

Reader Verdict

Readers agree on one thing: Plk deserved a wish on time. Rachit has been sentenced to remember every birthday from here on out.

In His Defence

He did make an entire newspaper. That counts for something, at least in theory.
